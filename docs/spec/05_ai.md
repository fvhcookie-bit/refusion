# 05. Opponents (AI)

## AI-1. The AI is a virtual gamepad [✓]
The AI does not move the ship directly. Every frame it sets the **same** actions and axes as the player's gamepad (SHIP-11):
throttle, steering −255…255, pitch, airbrakes, fire — and then the shared physics runs (03). The player's autopilot works the same way
(FLOW-10). In race recordings the bots show the same input combinations as a human (throttle + steering).

Consequence for the implementation: the AI and the physics can be built and verified separately; the "input → state" recording is the same
for all ships. Until the AI exists, bots can be driven with recorded input.

## AI-2. What an AI decision consists of [code]
In each frame, in order:
1. Recovery after a flip / flight (if needed).
2. A target on the **racing line** (AI-3).
3. Avoiding neighbours and attacking (attacking turns on at a high aggression level); the order depends on the lap and the pilot's flags.
4. Desired steering, throttle and brakes for the target.
5. Weapon: pickup, when to fire, when to discard (in Challenge or at aggression > 0).
   Quake: if any bot used it less than **5 s** (250 frames) ago, other bots do not use Quake
   (a shared ban on all bots; the timer runs every race frame). [code]
6. Writing the result into the ship's input.
From the third lap onward the behaviour changes (how — OQ-26). By the player's observations, bots differ noticeably in braking before corners depending on the ship and pilot; a bot that has flown off returns to the track at the nearest anchor point with zero speed, without a burst. No differences in recovery between weak and strong bots were noticed. [player observation]

## AI-3. Racing lines [code; line format — ✓]
The AI steers the ship along the layout lines recorded in a small `.SPL` file (FMT-24). The file contains several
lines, including the main racing line and the starting grid and pit lane lines. `.SPL` lines consist of linked
pieces with points and directions; their geometry and links must be consistent with each other, otherwise the AI may leave
the track. The exact meaning of some fields of the pieces and the boundaries remains open (OQ-27).

The link between the `.SPL` and the objects of the small `.TRI` file has not yet been established for the implementation; unconfirmed
decision points from the `.TRI` should not be taken as a source of AI behaviour.

## AI-4. Pilot character [code]
Static data — the pilot's INI (FMT-6), 20 values: aggression and its calming rate, alertness, concentration, smoothness,
skill level 1–3, rating at the start, reaction at the start (`stall`), a "grudge" against a specific pilot, flags
and 8 steering coefficients: `lookAheadFactor`, `minTurn`, `airbrakeTurn`, `brakeTurn`, `steeringFactor`, `slowDown`,
`sharpBend`, `err_mult` (steering error). How exactly each coefficient affects steering — OQ-26.

## AI-5. Aggression [code]
Every frame, bots only:
- grows when the pilot is being overtaken (their position got worse): +10 if overtaken by their "grudge holder" (`givenGrudge`), otherwise +5;
  not more than 100;
- every 0.5 s it calms down: `aggression −= min(agressionCalming·0.025, aggression − base)`, where base is the value from the INI.

## AI-6. Concentration and smoothness [code]
Smoothness changes on timers following the change in aggression; concentration does not change in this per-frame update.
The limits and exact conditions for changing the parameters, and the possible influence of concentration on other AI decisions, remain
open (OQ-26).

## AI-7. Strength of opponents
- The race's speed class sets the version of the ships for all participants (SHIP-14); the AI has upgrades no lower than the player's (SHIP-16),
  and they can be stronger (weapon damage multiplier up to 2.1 versus 1.1). [✓]
- Rubber band (RACE-7), AI start bursts (FLOW-6), the AI's weaker booster (PHY-11). [code]

## AI-8. Entering the pit lane [code; observations agree]
Where the racing line branches into the pit lane, the AI turns into the pit lane with a probability of `p` %:
- `p = (100 − shield_as_percent_of_full) × m`, where m = 0.6 / 0.8 / 1.0 for pilot skill level 1 / 2 / 3 (AI-4);
- `p = 100` if a long-lasting weapon effect that pit lane recharging removes is active on the ship (PHY-24);
- `p = 0` if the lap counter (RACE-3) ≥ `number_of_laps − 1` — that is, in a 3-lap race the AI enters only on lap 1;
- `p = 0` if the pit lane is disabled in the event.
In recordings all voluntary AI entries are on lap 1 of 3, with a shield of 25…75 % of full.
